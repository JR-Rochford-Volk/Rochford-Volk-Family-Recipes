## About My Collection

This is a collection of my family's recipes gathered from the Parish, O'Leary, Rochford, and Volk families.The purpose of this collection is preservation. Digitally preserving my family’s recipes has been a project idea passed around during family gatherings for about a decade, and this project is the perfect time to actually begin the process of gathering and preserving all of my family’s recipes. Aside from preserving these items, my family often request my mother take photos and send them. Several members of my family actively use these recipes. I find that I use the recipes at least once a month, and sifting through my past text messages to find the correct image is tedious.

My family are the primary audience for this collection, but my family has also shared these recipes with many non-family members. We are extremely proud of these recipes, which are most very unhealthy but extremely delicious. Like good food, recipes are meant to be shared.

Ultimately, my collection of family recipes is meant to provide my family with easy access to our shared history of cooking and eating great meals. This is the only collection that contain these recipes, which are unique to my family. No one has a complete holding of the recipes except for my mother, who enjoys sharing them, but the work of sharing is tedious.

## Course Requirement Details

This collection was created to fulfill requirements for Information Organization (SLIS:5030:0EXZ), a course taught by Dr. Stephanie Blalock at the Unviersity of Iowa. This is a core coure requirement to fulfill the requirements for the MLIS, which is accredited by the American Library Assocation.
